# INTERVIEW-COACH — Context Guide

## What it does

INTERVIEW-COACH gets a **human interviewer** ready to run a great interview — and
helps them score it afterward. It's the coach in your corner, not the interview
designer (that's INTERVIEW-LAB). Use it before you walk in, and again when you sit
down to write your feedback.

## What you get back

- **A pre-interview briefing** — your target competencies, 3-5 prepared questions
  with follow-up "ladders" to dig deeper, a **do-not-ask / ask-instead** list for
  the role, a timing plan, and one bias to watch for.
- **A debrief assist** — turns your rough notes into evidence-based rubric scores and
  flags where thin evidence or bias might be driving your call.
- **On-demand coaching** on any of the seven dimensions: legal guardrails, digging
  for signal, prep, narrative control, selling, scoring nuance, bias.

## What it needs from you

| Input | Required? | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Role + level | Required | "Senior Product Manager" |
| Which stage/competency you own | Required | "behavioral round", "systems design" |
| Your experience interviewing | Optional | "I do this maybe twice a year" |
| Jurisdiction / country | Optional | "California, US" (sharpens legal guidance) |
| A specific worry | Optional | "I run over time", "I warm to people like me" |

## How to use it well

- Tell it your **real worry** — that's where the coaching gets specific.
- For the legal list, give your **country/state** if you can; rules vary. And still
  run your company's official do-not-ask list past HR/legal — this is good-practice
  guidance, not legal advice.
- For a debrief, **paste your own rough notes** (keep them de-identified if the tool
  is shared) and let it help you map them to the rubric.

## Two important limits

- **Not legal advice.** Employment law varies and changes; defer to your legal/HR team.
- **No real recordings or candidate PII** in a shared Project/GPT. Analyzing real
  interview transcripts is a separate, private capability (work-machine only) — see
  the team that set this up.

## Start here

> "Brief me to run the behavioral round for a Senior Backend Engineer. I'm an
> occasional interviewer and I tend to run out of time."

> "Here are my rough notes from a Staff PM onsite — help me turn them into
> evidence-based rubric scores and tell me where I might be biased."
